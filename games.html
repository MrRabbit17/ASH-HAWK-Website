01 - Story

The Ashfall War

A kingdom shattered by volcanic ash and civil war is reborn under the shadow of old samurai clans. You are Hawkbound, a swordsman linked to a phoenix spirit that rises when honor is tested.

Dojo of Steel and Fire

剣術 Swordsmanship

Precision blade combat with stance switching and timing windows.

鳳凰の型 Phoenix Form

Use rising-phoenix techniques to counter, dash, and reclaim burned ground.

Critical story fights judged by your conduct, not only by victory.

Ash, Steel, and Consequence

Villages, shrines, and battlefields evolve from your decisions. Protect civilians and old temples to restore the land, or take ruthless paths that expand the ash front.
04 - Art Direction

Samurai Cinema Aesthetic

Inspired by classic samurai cinema and modern epic visuals: ink-like shadows, ember orange highlights, weather-driven atmosphere, and ritual architecture shaped by Japanese tradition.
