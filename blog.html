2026 — Vol. 03

ASHFALL — Combat Prototype Complete

The first playable combat prototype is live internally. We built a stance-switching system inspired by classic samurai cinema. Three stances, each with unique attack chains and parry windows. Early feedback confirms the "Phoenix Form" counter mechanic is our strongest hook. Next sprint: enemy AI behaviour trees and the first boss encounter.

2026 — Vol. 02

Art Direction — Samurai Cinema Meets Ink Wash

We finalised the visual identity for ASHFALL: a fusion of ink wash painting (sumi-e) and cinematic colour grading. Environments use a desaturated ash palette punctuated by ember-orange highlights. Character silhouettes are sharp, inspired by kabuki blocking. The goal is to make every frame feel like a painted scroll in motion.

Studio Launch — From Ashes, Worlds Rise

ASH HAWK Games is officially founded. Our mission: build story-driven action adventures rooted in Japanese-inspired atmosphere, bushido philosophy, and technical ambition.
